# Design QA — IACC Pastel Archive

## Result
Passed for the desktop preview. The page opens with a compact summary of the report’s argument, headline metrics, and policy criticism before the long-form sections. The metrics appear without an interpretive caveat box or text that qualifies the findings. The presentation stays within the attached archive art direction: warm ivory paper, dusty blush and powder-blue fields, muted botanical detail, editorial serif typography, and pastel report covers.

## Checks
- **Summary coverage:** names the process break and shift toward agency-led and therapeutic/medication priorities, and the report’s concerns about visibility, agency, member affiliations, representation, and disclosure.
- **Metric alignment:** autism references −61%; agency mentions 13.7×; 62.2% of fixed-vocabulary terms changed at least 2×; 68.8% of strong vocabulary trends reversed.
- **Tone:** uses attributed framing (“the page argues”) and concise narrative paragraphs; no bullet list.
- **Layout:** summary and criticism prose fit together in the checked desktop viewport before the full article.
- **Color and imagery:** muted ink, blush, powder blue, sage, and faded ochre on warm ivory; artwork has no visible distortion.
- **Interactions:** TL;DR navigation scrolls to the summary; primary CTA and chapter anchors work; term search filters sample terms and clearing restores the list.
- **Console:** no application errors observed; earlier metadata errors came from a browser extension.
- **Responsive coverage:** desktop reviewed; narrow mobile layout not captured in this pass.

## Scope
This is a separate static visual prototype with sample term-search data. It does not change the source repository or live site, and it does not port the full interactive explorer or dataset.
